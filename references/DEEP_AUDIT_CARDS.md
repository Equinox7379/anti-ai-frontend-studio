# 66 个成熟方案深读卡片

这里的“深读”基于官方详细文档、公开产品说明或获奖详情页；不等于登录后完整可用性测试。每张卡片同时记录可迁移原则和不可迁移边界。

## 01. Squarespace Foundations

- **参考 ID：** `AW-001`
- **来源类型：** award_site
- **页面任务：** Explain a brand system through an interactive experience
- **信息拓扑：** narrative / exploration
- **可迁移原则：** A restrained two-color budget can keep the identity legible while interaction carries the expressive load.
- **不可迁移／风险：** Do not transfer the scroll-led brand-tour structure into a dense work application.
- **证据范围：** detail page and metadata
- **来源：** https://www.awwwards.com/sites/squarespace-foundations
